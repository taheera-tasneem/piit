# 📊 Superstore Data Analysis Using Tableau

## Project Overview

This project uses **Tableau** to analyze the Superstore dataset and create interactive visualizations and dashboards. The analysis focuses on understanding **sales performance, profitability, customer orders, product performance, regional trends, and the impact of discounts**.

The goal of the project is to transform transactional sales data into meaningful business insights and demonstrate practical Tableau data visualization skills.

## Dataset

The **Superstore dataset** contains retail transaction data with information about:

* Orders
* Customers
* Products
* Categories and sub-categories
* Sales
* Profit
* Discounts
* Quantity
* Order and shipping dates
* Regions and states
* Customer segments

## Tool Used

* **Tableau**
* Data visualization
* Interactive dashboards
* Calculated fields
* Filters
* Parameters
* Sets
* Table calculations
* Level of Detail (LOD) expressions

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales Analysis

Sales performance was analyzed across different dimensions, including:

* Region
* Category
* Sub-category
* Customer segment
* Time

This helps identify areas generating the highest and lowest sales.

### 2. Profit Analysis

Profit was analyzed to identify:

* Most profitable categories and sub-categories
* Areas with low or negative profit
* Relationship between sales and profitability
* Regional differences in profit performance

### 3. Sales Trend Analysis

Time-series visualizations were created to examine how sales change over time.

This analysis helps identify:

* Sales growth
* Seasonal patterns
* High and low sales periods
* Overall sales trends

### 4. Discount vs. Profit Analysis

The relationship between **discount and profit** was analyzed to understand how discounting affects profitability.

This visualization helps identify products or transactions where higher discounts may be associated with lower profits.

### 5. Regional Analysis

Geographic and regional visualizations were used to compare sales and profit performance across different locations.

This helps identify:

* High-performing regions
* Low-performing regions
* Geographic differences in business performance

### 6. Pareto Analysis

A **Pareto chart** was created to analyze the cumulative contribution of products or sub-categories to overall sales.

This analysis helps identify which areas contribute the largest percentage of total business performance.

### 7. KPI Analysis

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) were used to summarize important business measures such as:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Number of Orders
* Profit Margin

KPIs provide a quick overview of overall business performance.
